Facilitator guide: 3 team workshops

A way to turn the concepts into short team exercises. Use sample data or information you have permission to share.

0140 MIN

Context and workflows

Each participant drafts a project context, identifies a redundant process and tries a verifiable question.

OUTPUT: First use case and the sources it needs.

0245 MIN

Decisions and prototypes

One group prepares a dashboard with sample data; another explores discovery and prototyping; technical participants suggest integrations and acceptance criteria.

OUTPUT: A demo with hypotheses and validation criteria.

0345 MIN

Routines and safeguards

Design an automation with least privilege, a human approver and a rollback plan; debate whether to scale it.

OUTPUT: Pilot proposal, risks and owner.

ASSESSMENT RUBRIC

Rate 1–5: usefulness, source quality, adoption effort, cost and risk. Compare ratings before making a decision.
